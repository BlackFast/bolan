Change types:

- `initial`: first durable shelf soul.
- `minor addition`: new book reinforces or extends the existing soul.
- `corrective addition`: new book corrects, narrows, or complicates old claims.
- `conflict addition`: new book creates a serious tension the soul must preserve.
- `major reframing`: the central question, category, or identity changes.

## Versioning Rules

- Start with `v1.0` for the first durable shelf soul.
- Use minor increments for normal additions: `v1.0` to `v1.1`.
- Use larger minor jumps for conflict-heavy additions: `v1.1` to `v1.5`.
- Use a major version when the central question, category, or identity changes: `v1.5` to `v2.0`.
- Keep old entries intact even when the current soul later changes direction.

## Evolution Quality Checklist

- The new book has its own profile before changing the soul.
- The belonging test is explicit.
- Old claims that changed are named.
- Reinforcement, correction, contradiction, and new vocabulary are separated.
- Internal friction is preserved.
- The version increment matches the size of the change.
- The final delta summary is understandable without rereading every profile.
